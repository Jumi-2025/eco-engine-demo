What this shows

A review gate before calculation, transparent arithmetic and a record of the approved inputs in the downloaded summary. Approvals are temporary browser interactions, not authenticated signatures.

What stays outside this demo

The full platform source, private records, uploads, accounts, production infrastructure and commercial materials. There are no upload controls, server connections or analytics in this example.
